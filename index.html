Our foundation

Business school by day, disciples by conviction.

CBSF exists so no student has to choose between their faith and their field. We are a fellowship of Business Administration students committed to living out the gospel in every ledger, pitch and negotiation.

“Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord.”Colossians 3:23

We believe business is a mission field—one that calls for integrity, courage and Christ-like leadership.
